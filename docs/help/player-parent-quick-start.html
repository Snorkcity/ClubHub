Player & parent quick start

Join the right team, find the schedule and let your coach know your availability.

Start with the right account

Use Nahreo in a browser on your iPhone, Android phone or computer. For these steps, you do not need an app-store download.

Players: use your own invitation and account.
Parents and guardians: use your own adult account, linked to your player. Do not sign in as your child or accept the child's invitation using your own email. A linked child does not need their own login for you to manage their attendance.
Joining as a parent: ask your club administrator for a Parent / Guardian invitation linked to your child. Use your own invited email and your own account, then choose Complete invitation. The family link becomes active after acceptance. Do not accept a Player or Manager role just to join as a parent, or create a second player using your own email.

1. Join with a team link or code

  1. Open the reusable team link shared by your coach, or choose Join a team with a code in Nahreo and enter the code.
  2. Check the club and team name. Sign in with your existing Nahreo account, even if you already belong to another team or club.
  3. New users can create an account. Continue with Google normally avoids a separate Nahreo verification email. Email-and-password sign-up still requires email verification.
  4. Choose Player to join as yourself. Parents must choose Parent / Guardian, enter their child's name and wait for a coach to confirm the link to the correct player.

No invitation email needs to be opened when using a shared team link or code. Guardian access to a child's reports and attendance begins only after approval. Ask the coach if your request is still waiting.

Using a personal invitation instead

  1. Open the secure team invitation from your coach or club administrator. Check the team name.
  2. If you already use Nahreo, choose Sign in and join. Keep your existing account, even if you also belong to another team or club.
  3. If you are new, choose New to Nahreo? Create an account.
  4. Use the email address the invitation was made for and complete any verification requested.
  5. Choose Complete invitation, then check that the correct club and team are selected.

The link expires after seven days. If it is unavailable, ask for a new one. Signing in without completing the invitation does not automatically join the team.

2. Parents: confirm your linked player

  1. Open your own Parent / Guardian invitation, not your child's invitation.
  2. Choose Sign in and join if you already use Nahreo, or New to Nahreo? Create an account. Use your own invited email and complete verification.
  3. Choose Complete invitation to activate the family link.
  4. Check that the correct club and team are selected.
  5. Open Schedule, choose an event your player is invited to, and check that their name appears in the attendance controls. Parents do not need the staff-only Team Members menu.
  6. If your player is missing, contact the club administrator. They need to check the family link and your permission to manage that player.
Do not create a new player profile to solve a missing-link problem. Your player's existing profile holds their team history.

3. Find the next event

  1. Open Schedule.
  2. Open the next training session or game for your selected team.
  3. Read the date, start or kick-off time, arrival instructions, location and any notes.

If you belong to more than one team, check the selected team before assuming an event is missing.

4. Tell your coach who can attend

Players: use the event's attendance controls to respond for yourself.

Parents and guardians: find RSVP for your children and use the controls showing your player's name. Choose Going or Not going for that child, not for you.

  1. Choose the response that matches your player's availability.
  2. If asked for an absence reason, add a brief explanation.
  3. Check that the saved response appears against the correct player's name.
  4. If your plans change, return to the event and update the response.
Managing more than one player? Check each name and respond separately. If you cannot see a response control for your child, ask the club administrator to check your family link and management permission. Some events are for staff only.

5. Read your team's welcome post

Open Home and look under Team News for the coach's welcome post.

Check Schedule as well as Team News: reading a post is not a replacement for saving your attendance response on the event.

Draft limitation: the new team's chat did not appear in Messages during the walkthrough. Team News and Messages are different features; this guide does not yet verify joining or reading a new team chat.

What about check-ins and reports?

Your coach will explain when your team should use extra features. Player RPE, wellness and learning preferences start at U14; younger teams will not show the same options.

Learning preferences are a self-reflection questionnaire, not a diagnosis or a fixed label. Player feedback reports become available only after staff review and release them.

Quick help

  • My invitation will not open: ask your coach or administrator for a new link.
  • I signed in but cannot see my team: check the invited email, finish the invitation and check the selected club and team.
  • I cannot see my child: ask the club administrator to check the adult-to-player family link. Do not use the child's account instead.
  • I cannot find an event: check the selected team and ask your coach whether you or your player were invited.
  • I cannot respond for my child: check you are looking at that child's named attendance controls, then ask the administrator to check management permission.
