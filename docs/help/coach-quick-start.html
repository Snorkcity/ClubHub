Coach quick start

Get into your team, organise your first event and welcome your players.

Before you start

These steps work in a phone or desktop browser. You need an invitation to the correct team and access to the email address it was sent to.

For a new team in an existing club: your club administrator creates the team and invites its coaches. Signing in alone does not create or join a team. Setting up an entirely new club requires approval from the Nahreo owner.

1. Accept your coach invitation

  1. Open the secure invitation link from your club administrator. Check the team name and that your invited role is coach.
  2. Already use Nahreo? Choose Sign in and join and use your existing account. Otherwise choose New to Nahreo? Create an account.
  3. Use the email address the invitation was created for and complete any verification requested.
  4. Choose Complete invitation. Check the club and selected team before adding anything.

An invitation expires after seven days. If it is unavailable, ask for a fresh link. Do not create another account just because your team is missing.

2. Check your team and invite players

  1. On Home, press the club logo at the top left to select your team or switch to another club you belong to. After switching clubs, use the logo again to choose a team in that club. Ask the club administrator to correct any team-name or age-group issues.
  2. Open Team Members, then choose Invite someone. On desktop, Team Members is in the sidebar. On a phone, open your profile/avatar menu and choose Team Members.
  3. Check Team, enter the person's name and their own email, and choose the appropriate Team role. Use Player for a player, not for a parent.
  4. Choose Send by email or Create link only. With link-only delivery, copy the secure invitation link and share it privately with the intended person.
Seeing a person on the roster does not prove they have completed sign-in and accepted their invitation. Ask your first invited coach or player to confirm that they can open the team.

Children without their own email: a club administrator can open the team's Player Roster → Add Player → New player without email, enter the child's name, then choose Add to team. Check the directory first to avoid duplicates. The player has their own permanent record; do not enter a parent's email as the child's email.

Reusable links and codes

Open Team Members with the correct team selected, then use Join code & guardian requests → Create code. Copy the link or code and share it through your usual messaging app. Players can sign in and join directly; no invitation email is needed. Google sign-in normally avoids a separate Nahreo verification email; email-and-password sign-up retains verification.

Keep an eye on the team's membership list. Use Replace code or Disable code if the link has spread too widely. These actions stop old links from working but do not remove people who have already joined. Staff permissions still require an individual invitation.

Parents can choose Parent / Guardian and enter their child's name. Review their request in this same area, select the correct existing player, then approve or reject it. Approval gives access to that child's private reports and attendance, so confirm the relationship before approving. Add the child's permanent player record first if it is not already on the roster.

3. Arrange access for parents and guardians

Parents and children are separate people in Nahreo. A guardian must be linked to the correct player before they can manage that player's attendance.

Ask your club administrator to arrange guardian access:

  1. Add or select the correct child on the team's player roster.
  2. Open Team Members → Invite someone and choose that team.
  3. Enter the adult's name and email, choose Parent / Guardian under Team role, then select Player they look after.
  4. Send the invitation by email, or choose Create link only and share the secure link privately.
  5. The adult signs in or registers with the invited email and chooses Complete invitation. They use their existing Nahreo account if they already have one.
  6. After acceptance, check the family link and a named-child attendance response.
The family link becomes active when the adult accepts. This does not make them a Player, Coach or Manager. Club admins can also open a player's profile from Team Members, then use Family → Link guardian to link existing club people.

4. Create your first event

  1. Open Schedule and use the create-event control.
  2. Check the Team and event Type. Give it a clear Title.
  3. Set the date, time, location and any arrival instructions. Check who is included under Invitations; include players when you want player attendance responses.
  4. Choose Create event, then choose whether to notify the invited people. For a setup check, use Create without notifying.
  5. Open the saved event and check the details. Ask one player or linked guardian to respond before inviting the whole team.

The selected-team Schedule view shows that team's events. If something seems missing, first check the selected club and team.

5. Publish a short welcome post

With the correct team selected, open Home and choose Write post. Add a clear title and a short welcome with the first training details, then publish it. Check that the post appears in Team News.

Suggested message
Welcome to our team on Nahreo. Please check the first training event in Schedule and add your availability. Parents: if you cannot see your player, contact the club administrator so we can check the family link.
Separate launch check: a newly created team's conversation did not appear in Messages during the walkthrough, for either the coach or guardian. A Team News post is not a chat message. Resolve new-team chat setup before relying on Messages for a new team.

Ready for your team?

  • The right club, team and age group are selected.
  • A second coach or player has accepted an invitation successfully.
  • Your first event has the correct time, location and invitees.
  • A player or managing guardian can respond to that event.
  • The team can find the welcome post in Team News.
  • A newly invited parent has accepted and can see the correct child.
  • New-team chat setup has been resolved before relying on Messages.
  • You know who will help families with account or linking problems.

Age-based features: player RPE, wellness and learning preferences start at U14. Junior match timekeeping is available through U17, not U18 or senior teams. Coaches keep their own learning-preferences questionnaire.

If someone gets stuck

  • Invitation unavailable: request a new link from the person who invited them.
  • Wrong account or email: sign in with the invited email. If the invitation used the wrong address, ask the administrator to correct it.
  • Missing team or event: check the selected club and team, then confirm the invitation was completed.
  • Missing player for a parent: ask the club administrator to check the adult's account and family link. Do not create a duplicate player.
