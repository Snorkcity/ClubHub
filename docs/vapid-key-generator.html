Squadly phone notification keys

This page generates a standard P-256 VAPID key pair entirely inside your browser.

Private: nothing is uploaded or stored. Keep the private key secret and never paste it into chat.

After mailto:, enter a monitored club email address.

Where to save them

  1. Add all three values to Replit Secrets for development.
  2. Add the exact same names and values to Railway Variables for production.
  3. Do not regenerate after phones subscribe unless you intend to make every device re-enable notifications.
