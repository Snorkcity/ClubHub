---
name: Market-entry pricing
description: Provisional commercial direction to revisit after Nahreo has real pilot usage.
---

Treat **$99 per team per season** as the leading future price for Nahreo Pro,
including wellbeing monitoring, reports, and no advertising. Consider a
time-limited founding-club offer around **$79 per team per season** to encourage
early adoption without permanently anchoring the product at a low price.

**Why:** Nahreo aims to compete on a calmer coordination experience plus useful
player wellbeing monitoring, not by being the cheapest product. Comparable
coordination products can cost around $140 per team per season without the same
wellbeing proposition.

**How to apply:** Keep this as a hypothesis rather than published pricing.
Validate coach usage, retention, willingness to pay, support costs, season
definitions, and club volume discounts before launch pricing is finalized.
