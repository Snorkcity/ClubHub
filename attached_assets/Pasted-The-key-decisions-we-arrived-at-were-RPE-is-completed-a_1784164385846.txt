The main references we discussed were based on the approach used by the Australian Institute of Sport (AIS) and high-performance environments generally:

Daily wellness questionnaires should be very short (around 30–60 seconds).
Session RPE should be collected 15–30 minutes after training or a match, rather than immediately at the final whistle, to improve consistency.
Wellness and training load are more valuable when trended over time than as isolated values.
Coaches should focus on changes from an athlete's normal baseline, not just absolute scores.
Combining internal load (RPE) with external load (GPS or minutes) provides a much clearer picture of readiness than either alone.
